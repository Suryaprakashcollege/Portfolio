Frequently Asked Questions

What is your core expertise in Supply Chain and Procurement?

I specialize in the complete Procure-to-Pay (P2P) lifecycle, encompassing purchase requisition (PR) creation, purchase order (PO) processing, strategic sourcing, RFQ analysis, and supplier contract negotiations. I support high-mix, mission-critical manufacturing operations for global and US-based clients like Sienna Corporation.

Which ERP systems and analytical tools do you use daily?

I manage daily procurement operations in SAP MM (Material Management), handling vendor master data, PO tracking, and goods receipt coordination. In addition, I build advanced MS Excel data models using XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, and dynamic Pivot Tables for price benchmarking, landed cost evaluations, and shortage analytics.

How do you achieve 4–6% cost savings consistently?

Through structured RFQ benchmarking across authorized global distributors (including DigiKey and Mouser), aggregating purchase volumes, negotiating competitive lead times and payment terms, and implementing Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) arrangements to reduce holding and freight overhead.

How does your engineering and automation background support supply chain?

With a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication and practical experience in software automation testing (Java, Selenium, Cypress), I possess a deep technical understanding of electronic components and schematics, alongside a rigorous algorithmic approach to workflow automation and data verification.

What is your experience with international logistics and US client management?

I work directly with US client stakeholders in real-time, coordinating international shipments with global carriers including DHL, FedEx, and UPS. I manage customs compliance, track transit milestones, and ensure zero line disruptions through proactive shortage mitigation.

What roles are you targeting and what is your location?

I am targeting roles in Supply Chain Management, Procurement Engineering, Strategic Sourcing, and Vendor Operations. Based in Chennai, India, I am open to immediate opportunities supporting international manufacturing and enterprise supply chains.
